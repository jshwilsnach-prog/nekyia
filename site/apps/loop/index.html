1 to i to 0, in a loop

Three stations, no finish line. Each walker goes round at its own rate, which is not a rank — a faster circle is not a better circle. When two of them reach a station together the room draws the meeting and, if you let it, sounds it.

The rates are deliberately incommensurate, so the pattern never repeats exactly and no walker ever laps the others into a standing. Meetings happen because two paths coincide for a moment, not because anyone earned one. 1 is relation and rings clear. i is the whole and sits orthogonal — it is detuned against everything on purpose, and it never resolves. 0 is rank, and it is silent, because that is what rank is worth here.

Sound needs a tap first; browsers require a gesture before audio, and this page is not going to work around that. No recording, no microphone, no storage.
