A star that will not construct

Compass and unmarked straightedge refuse it. The twelve-month sky refuses it. Seven will not sit in three hundred and sixty. Sight can count seven lamps. It cannot construct the angle.

By will lets you drag each lamp. Nothing snaps, nothing scores, nothing is wrong. By sight lays them at 360/n, which for seven is 51.428571… — a repeat with no last digit, the same refusal the compass makes. A construction is exact or it is not. Deviation is shown because it is true, not because it is a mark against you.

Constructibility is Gauss–Wantzel: a regular n-gon yields to compass and unmarked straightedge exactly when n is a power of two times distinct Fermat primes (3, 5, 17, 257, 65537). Seven is none of these. Neither is nine, eleven, or thirteen.
