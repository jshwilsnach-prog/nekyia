A date and a whole number

Aught is proof of work that links to Nekyia. Opening the play door is the work. What comes out is a UTC date and a whole number — never an address, never a key, never a tip total, never a person. Even a perfect proof would not name a walker.

What this refuses

There is no field for a name here, and there will not be one. No address, no viewing key, no email, no handle, no wallet. Nothing is sent anywhere — the hashing runs in this tab on your own processor, and the page makes no network request at all once it has loaded. Nothing is written to storage. Reload and today's work is gone, which costs you a few seconds and costs a tally everything it wanted.

An opening is not a login and not a rank. Two walkers who both opened the door today hold the same thing. A walker who opened it a thousand times holds the same thing. asRank(anything) is 0, and that is not a consolation, it is the design.

Why work at all

Because a cost that cannot be pointed at a person is the only kind worth asking for. The statement is there exists an opening. The witness — which nonce, on which machine, by whose hands — stays with you. A homomorphic tally of these would still be a number, not a who. Do not store a richer number because it is encrypted.
