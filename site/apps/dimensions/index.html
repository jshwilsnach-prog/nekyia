One walk, five ways

The walking holds in 3D, 2D, 1D, 0D, and a loop. Same corridors, same lamps, same law. Dropping a dimension is not a graphics setting and not a downgrade — it is a different relation to the same place.

Arrows or WASD. There is no exit, no timer, and no door at the end. Seven lamps are in here. You can count them; counting them ranks nothing and nobody, and the tally resets when you leave because it was never a record of you. In 0D there is no view at all — only what you stand in relation to, which is the part every other dimension was decorating.
